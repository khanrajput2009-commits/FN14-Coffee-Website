About FN14 Coffee

Born from late-night study sessions and a love of a really good cup.

Our brand story

FN14 started as a tiny roasting table and a shared obsession with flavour. Today we serve thousands of cups, still roasting in small batches and still checking every recipe by hand.

Taste the story

Mission

Make exceptional coffee an easy, joyful part of everyday life.

Vision

To be the most trusted coffee brand for people who care about craft.

Selected beans

We buy Arabica from eight regions, chosen for sweetness, body and traceability. Only lots that pass our cupping table reach your cup.

Careful preparation

Grind to order, brew at 93°C, steam milk to silk. Every drink follows a tested recipe.

Our journey

2020 Launched home delivery across the city.
2023 Introduced the FN14 Signature Coffee.
2026 Opened our online store with 12,000+ cups served.
